Airbnb Listings, 8 More US Cities

90,746 rows8 cities12 columnsfree full data

Eight US short-term-rental markets the six-city pack doesn't cover: Boston, Chicago, Los Angeles, New Orleans, Portland, San Francisco, Seattle, and Washington DC. Same 12 columns per listing as the first pack: price, room type, neighborhood, approximate location, minimum nights, availability for the next 365 days, review count and rate, and last review date. Snapshots are Inside Airbnb releases from June 14 to 24, 2026, and the full CSVs download free, one file per city.

The two packs are nearly twins in size: 90,746 rows here against 90,169 there. Los Angeles is the biggest single-city file on the site at 43,932 listings, ahead of New York City's 30,234; Portland is the smallest here at 4,210. City medians run from USD 160 in Portland to USD 281 in Seattle; the combined median across all 8 cities is USD 226.

Room mix: Entire home/apt 67,526 (74%), Private room 21,402 (24%), Hotel room 1,371 (2%), Shared room 447. Boston's rows skew hotel-style private rooms, which is why its median (279) sits above Los Angeles (224) despite LA's long tail of 20,000-plus outliers. San Francisco's 99,999 top price is a source artifact; nothing is edited out. 11,594 rows (13%) carry no price, and every median here counts price-bearing rows only.

Built from the Inside Airbnb summary files: host id, host name, and licence columns dropped, everything else kept verbatim. The full column dictionary, per-city snapshot dates, and caveats are in schema.md.

Source: Inside Airbnb, CC BY 4.0. Snapshots: Boston 2026-06-15, Chicago 2026-06-24, Los Angeles 2026-06-15, New Orleans 2026-06-16, Portland 2026-06-15, San Francisco 2026-06-14, Seattle 2026-06-15, Washington DC 2026-06-24.
