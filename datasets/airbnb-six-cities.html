Airbnb Listings, 6 US Cities

90,169 rows8 tables2026-08-10one-time full pack

Six US short-term-rental markets in one normalized schema, 12 columns per listing: price, room type, neighborhood, location (blurred to about 11 meters), minimum nights, availability for the next 365 days, review count and rate, and last review date. Austin, Nashville, Denver, New York City, Las Vegas (Clark County), and San Diego, snapshot dates June through August 2026.

cities-index.csv gives one row per city with row counts, median nightly price, and room-type mix, so you can compare markets before opening the big files. New York City alone contributes 30,234 rows; Austin's median sits at USD 217 a night.

Built from Inside Airbnb summary data. Host names and exact coordinates stripped, prices cleaned from the source text, one row per listing per city.

Source: Inside Airbnb, CC BY 4.0. Snapshots: Austin 2026-06-22, Nashville 2026-06-26, Denver 2026-06-30, Las Vegas 2026-06-27, San Diego 2026-06-27, New York City 2026-08-10.

Samples and full pack: data from Inside Airbnb, CC BY 4.0.
