Eurostat House Price Index, NL & EU27

342 rows2005-2025 annualNL + EU27 geosfree full CSV

Eurostat's annual house price index (dataset code prc_hpi_a) pulled for the Netherlands and the EU-27 aggregate, cleaned into one flat CSV. Six columns: year, geo, purchase (total / newly built / existing dwellings), unit, value, plus human-readable labels for geo, purchase, and unit.

Three unit variants per row group: annual average index 2015=100, annual average index 2025=100, and annual rate of change in percent. Read the 2015=100 total-dwelling series and the NL/EU gap jumps out: both sit at 100.0 in 2015, but by 2025 the Netherlands is at 215.15 while EU-27 is at 161.93. Dutch prices grew 115% over the decade against 62% for the EU aggregate; in 2021 the gap was widest, NL +14.5% against EU-27's +8.4%, and in 2025 NL rose 8.5% against 5.5%.

Use it to benchmark European housing against the US series in the HUD FMR datasets, backtest rent-vs-price models against the Eurostat actual rents dataset, or track how the labour cycle lines up with prices in the Eurostat unemployment dataset. The ETL that produced it (tools/etl-eurostat.py) is a stdlib-only JSON-stat 2.0 parser, rerunnable against any Eurostat table code.

Source: Eurostat, dataset prc_hpi_a (house price index - annual data), last updated 2026-07-02, pulled 2026-09-10 via the Eurostat dissemination API. No key, no registration.
