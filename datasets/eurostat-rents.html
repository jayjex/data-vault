Eurostat HICP Actual Rents, NL & EU27

580 rows, 2 tables2009-2025 monthlyNL + EU27 geosfree full CSVs

Eurostat's Harmonised Index of Consumer Prices tracks actual rentals paid for a main residence under coicop code CP0411. This dataset pulls that slice for the Netherlands and the EU-27 aggregate into two flat CSVs. Both share the same six columns: month (YYYY-MM), geo, unit, value, plus labels for geo and unit.

rent-index-monthly.csv is the monthly index, 2015=100 (dataset code prc_hicp_midx): 302 rows, NL from 2009-12, EU-27 from 2016-12. rent-annual-rate.csv is the year-on-year rate of change in percent (prc_hicp_manr): 278 rows, NL from 2010-12, EU-27 from 2017-12. Eurostat computes the two series separately, so they don't share one start date.

The series show how sticky European rents are next to sale prices. December 2025 year-on-year: NL +4.9%, EU-27 +3.2%. Full index path: NL rents rose 32.4% since 2015 (index 132.36 at 2025-12) against 20.4% for EU-27 (120.36). Compare with the house price index dataset, where NL prices more than doubled over the same decade: Dutch rents moved at less than a third of the pace of Dutch sale prices.

Use it to benchmark the US rent series in the HUD FMR datasets, calibrate long-run rent-growth assumptions in models, or put rents next to the labour market in the Eurostat unemployment dataset. The ETL (tools/etl-eurostat.py) is a stdlib-only JSON-stat 2.0 parser, rerunnable against any Eurostat table code.

Source: Eurostat, datasets prc_hicp_midx and prc_hicp_manr (HICP - monthly data, index / annual rate of change), coicop CP0411, last updated 2026-02-06, pulled 2026-09-10 via the Eurostat dissemination API. No key, no registration.
