Eurostat Monthly Unemployment Rate by Sex, EU27 + 8 Countries

7,641 rows2003-01 to 2026-07 monthly9 geosSA seasonally adjustedfree full CSV

The annual pack in this catalog (Eurostat unemployment rate by sex and age) runs through 2025. Eurostat's monthly table une_rt_m goes further: this CSV carries the seasonally adjusted monthly rates through July 2026, the freshest Eurostat labour-market numbers on the site (source dataset updated 2026-09-04). Same flat shape as the annual file: month, geo, sex, age band, value, plus Eurostat's labels for each code.

The slice is unit PC_ACT (percent of the labour force, ILO definition), adjustment SA, age band TOTAL (16-74), both sexes plus males and females separately, for the EU-27 aggregate and eight countries. The grid is complete: 9 geos x 283 months x 3 sexes = 7,641 rows with no gaps. The euro area aggregate EA20 is not published in une_rt_m on the API (the request returns an empty cube), so it lives only in the annual dataset.

What the file shows in 2026-07 (total): EU-27 6.1%, with Spain 10.0% at the top of the nine geos and Poland 3.4% at the bottom. Females above males in 6 of 9 geos that month (EU-27 6.3 vs 5.9). The crisis peak is visible month by month: Greece total 28.3% in 2013-07, Spain 26.4% in 2013-02, EU-27 11.7% in 2013-05, against 9.8% when the series opens in 2003-01. Poland's 20.0% peak is older, 2003-08.

Use it for the months the annual file can't see: turning points, revision tracking, or joining monthly EU rates against the housing cycle in the house price index dataset and HICP rents dataset. For the long-run annual view with seven age bands including youth, see the annual unemployment dataset; for a guided read of both, see the labour market data guide. The ETL (tools/etl-eurostat-une-monthly.py) is the same stdlib-only JSON-stat 2.0 parser as the annual one, rerunnable.

Source: Eurostat, dataset une_rt_m (Unemployment by sex and age - monthly data), last updated 2026-09-04, pulled 2026-09-10 via the Eurostat dissemination API. No key, no registration.
