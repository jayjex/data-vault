Eurostat Unemployment Rate by Sex and Age, EU27 + 9 Countries

3,768 rows2003-2025 annual10 geos7 age bandsfree full CSV

Eurostat's harmonised unemployment series (une_rt_a, LFS definition) as one flat CSV: annual rates in percent of the labour force (unit PC_ACT) for the EU-27 aggregate, the euro area, and eight countries, from 2003 to 2025. Eight columns per row: year, geo, sex, age band, value, plus Eurostat's labels for each code.

The slice covers both sexes plus males and females separately, across seven age bands from Y15-24 up to Y55-74. Not every combination exists for every year - the aggregates start in 2005, the Netherlands enters in 2009, and a few early youth series are thin - so missing cells are absent rows rather than nulls.

What the file shows: the EU-27 headline rate (Y15-74, total) sat at 6.0% in 2025, half its 2013 crisis peak of 11.6%. Spain is the outlier among the eight countries at 10.5% against Poland's 3.1%. The 2013 youth extremes are still the record values in the file: Greece Y15-24 hit 64.6% and Spain 55.5%, against an EU-27 youth rate of 15.2% in 2025. The sex split runs the other way in 2025: EU-27 female unemployment 6.2% vs male 5.8%.

Use it to benchmark the US employment side of the HUD FMR datasets, feed macro models a long-run EU labour series, or compare unemployment with the housing cycle in the house price index dataset and HICP rents dataset. For a guided read of the numbers, see the labour market data guide. The ETL (tools/etl-eurostat-une.py) is a stdlib-only JSON-stat 2.0 parser, rerunnable against any Eurostat table code.

Source: Eurostat, dataset une_rt_a (Unemployment by sex and age - annual data), last updated 2026-06-11, pulled 2026-09-10 via the Eurostat dissemination API. No key, no registration.
