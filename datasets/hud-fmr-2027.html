HUD Fair Market Rents FY2027

51,871 ZIP rows3,229 counties52 states5 bedroom sizes

The catalog's FY2027 entry for HUD fair market rents, effective October 1, 2026. The source workbooks are FY27_FMRs.xlsx for county-level values and fy2027_safmrs.xlsx for ZIP-level ones; parsed, they give 51,871 ZIP-level rows across 38,601 ZIP codes, 3,229 county rows, and 52 states and territories, ten columns with dollar rents for studios through 4 bedrooms. The national 2BR median across all rows is 1,190 dollars, and the 25th-to-75th percentile band runs 1,020 to 1,590.

This page serves the free files directly: a 22-row ZIP sample (one HUD area block, Abilene, TX MSA) and the complete 52-row state summary. The summary shows the spread in one table: California tops the 2BR median at 2,600 dollars with a 5,260 ceiling in Santa Cruz-Watsonville ZIPs, Massachusetts follows at 2,550 and Hawaii at 2,535, while Arkansas and Mississippi tie for the cheapest mainland states at 960. Puerto Rico prices lower at 610. Texas carries the most ZIP rows of any state, 3,244.

The full FY2027 CSVs (ZIP, county, state, plus the data dictionary and build script) download free from Zenodo under DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22649503, citable and without signup. For the files themselves, the FY2027 ZIP dataset serves the whole 51,871-row file and the FY2027 metro dataset rolls the ZIPs up to the 50 largest metro areas. What changed year over year is the subject of the HUD FMR 2027 guide: the median 2BR moved +3.30%, and the steepest jumps and cuts are ZIP-level.

Source: US Department of Housing and Urban Development, FY2027 Fair Market Rent release, effective October 1, 2026. US government data, public domain; the Zenodo repackaging is CC BY 4.0. Data Vault is not affiliated with HUD.

The full files download free from Zenodo (CC BY 4.0 repackaging of public-domain HUD data, no attribution required to republish the rows). The FY2027 ZIP dataset keeps the whole 51,871-row file and its integrity checks, the FY2027 metro dataset covers the top-50 metro medians, the county FY2026 dataset and the ZIP-level FY2026 dataset cover the prior year. To read the data, the most-affordable-ZIPs guide ranks the cheapest mainland ZIPs, the SQL guide queries this exact file in DuckDB and SQLite, the bedroom-size deltas guide diffs the FY2026 and FY2027 medians, and the FY2027 guide walks the year-over-year change.
