OpenStreetMap Civic Amenities: Lagos, Nairobi, Addis Ababa

2,240 features, 3 cities3 amenity types2026-09-10 countedfree CSV

One CSV, nine rows: Lagos, Nairobi, and Addis Ababa × three civic amenity types. Each row is the count of OpenStreetMap features carrying that amenity tag inside the city's administrative boundary, queried from the Overpass API in one run, with the license line carried verbatim in a fourth column.

The amenity tags are amenity=drinking_water (fountains and public tap points), amenity=public_bookcase (street-side book-sharing shelves), and amenity=bicycle_repair_station (public stands with tools or an air pump). The query shape is area(id)->.a; nwr["amenity"="drinking_water"](area.a); out count;, one call per theme per city, area-filtered rather than bbox-filtered, so the numbers follow exact city limits.

Nairobi owns this comparison: 2,174 mapped drinking-water features, the largest single-city count in the catalog's whole OSM civic series, ahead of Tokyo (2,163) and Madrid (2,058). These are not ornamental fountains. Most are unnamed tap points, with a dense cluster through Kibera mapped by water and sanitation projects, which is exactly what public tap mapping looks like when the infrastructure is scarce. Addis Ababa has 5 water points and Lagos 60, most of the Lagos count one branded "Clean Water" tap cluster. Not one public bookcase is mapped in any of the three boundaries, and bicycle repair stands appear exactly once, a shop in Nairobi called gitonga bicycle services.

Counts ran on 2026-09-10 against the Overpass API (maps.mail.ru mirror, keyless, base snapshot 2026-09-08T19:24:39Z), so the numbers are a snapshot of a database that moves daily. A parallel run on the kumi.systems mirror (four months staler) matched 8 of 9 counts exactly; Nairobi drinking_water differed by one edit. Boundaries are Nominatim administrative relations, each passed to Overpass as its 3600000000-offset area ID: Lagos State R3718182, Nairobi County R3492709, and the Addis Ababa city administration R1707699.

For the same three amenity types elsewhere in the catalog, see the OSM US civic amenities dataset (New York, Los Angeles, Chicago, Austin, Seattle), the OSM Asia civic amenities dataset (Tokyo, Jakarta, Seoul), the OSM EU civic amenities dataset (Berlin, Amsterdam, Paris, Madrid, Warsaw), the OSM Latin America civic amenities dataset (São Paulo, Buenos Aires, Mexico City), the OSM MENA civic amenities dataset (Istanbul, Cairo, Mumbai), the OSM Oceania civic amenities dataset (Sydney, Melbourne, Auckland, with a drinking-water-toilets-bench trio), and the OSM US civic amenities, New York-Chicago-Seattle dataset (exact city boundaries, drinking water, toilets, benches).
