OpenStreetMap Civic Amenities: Tokyo, Jakarta, Seoul

2,489 nodes, 3 cities3 amenity types2026-09-10 countedfree CSV

One CSV, nine rows: three cities × three civic amenity types. For each pair, the total count of OpenStreetMap nodes inside the official admin boundary, plus one example node with its name, latitude, and longitude so you can spot-check the mapping practice in that city.

The amenity tags are amenity=drinking_water (public fountains and tap points), amenity=public_bookcase (street-side book-sharing shelves), and amenity=bicycle_repair_station (public stands with tools or an air pump). Nodes only, not ways, which is how these three tags are overwhelmingly mapped.

The contrast between cities is the point of the dataset. Tokyo has 2,163 mapped drinking water points against Jakarta's 38, and that gap says as much about how actively each city's OSM community maps amenities as it does about infrastructure. Jakarta's public_bookcase count is a flat 0: nobody has mapped one yet inside the DKI Jakarta boundary, which is a real result, not missing data. Seoul sits between, with 253 fountains that often carry the 아리수 (Arisu) brand name in the tags.

City boundaries resolved from OSM relations by way of Nominatim: Tokyo Metropolis (relation 1543125), Daerah Khusus Ibukota Jakarta (6362934), Seoul Special City (2297418). Counting ran on 2026-09-10 via Overpass API queries of the form area->a; node["amenity"="drinking_water"](area.a); out count;, so the numbers are a snapshot: OSM moves daily.

The same three amenity types are counted for a different set of cities in the OSM US civic amenities dataset, so the Asia and America columns are directly comparable. For a European cut of open civic data, see the OSM EU civic amenities dataset. And the newest region in the series is Africa: OSM Africa civic amenities dataset (Lagos, Nairobi, Addis Ababa). The OSM Oceania civic amenities dataset. covers Sydney, Melbourne, and Auckland, and the OSM US New York-Chicago-Seattle civic amenities dataset counts the same trio inside exact city limits with a drinking-water-toilets-bench trio.
