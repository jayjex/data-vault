OpenStreetMap Civic Amenities: Berlin, Amsterdam, Paris, Madrid, Warsaw

4,669 nodes, 5 cities3 amenity types2026-09-10 countedfree CSV

One CSV, fifteen rows: Berlin, Amsterdam, Paris, Madrid, and Warsaw × three civic amenity types. Each row is the count of OpenStreetMap nodes carrying that amenity tag inside the city's administrative boundary, queried from the Overpass API in one snapshot, with the license line carried verbatim in a fourth column.

The amenity tags are amenity=drinking_water (fountains and public tap points), amenity=public_bookcase (street-side book-sharing shelves), and amenity=bicycle_repair_station (public stands with tools or an air pump). The query shape is area(id)->.a; node["amenity"="drinking_water"](area.a); out count;, one call per theme per city, area-filtered rather than bbox-filtered, so the numbers follow exact city limits.

Madrid leads drinking water at 2,058 mapped nodes, more than the other four cities combined, and Paris adds 1,045. The bookcase column swings the other way: Amsterdam maps zero public bookcases (the tag never caught on there, not an absence of shelves), Paris has 165, and Berlin 114. Bike repair stations are rare except in Warsaw, whose 188 stands are more than the other four cities combined, which tracks with a city where the bike-share network and its repair infrastructure were rebuilt in the 2010s.

Counts ran on 2026-09-10 against the Overpass API (kumi.systems mirror, base snapshot 2026-05-31), so the numbers are a snapshot of a database that moves daily. Boundaries are Nominatim administrative relations (Berlin R62422, Amsterdam R271110, Paris R71525, Madrid R5326784, Warsaw R336075), each passed to Overpass as its 3600000000-offset area ID.

For the same three amenity types in New York, Los Angeles, Chicago, Austin, and Seattle, see the OSM US civic amenities dataset, or the Tokyo, Jakarta, and Seoul counts in the OSM Asia civic amenities dataset. For open EU macro data in the same catalog, the Eurostat house price index dataset and the Eurostat actual rents dataset cover NL and EU-27 housing. For the African counts (Lagos, Nairobi, Addis Ababa), see the OSM Africa civic amenities dataset. The OSM Oceania civic amenities dataset. covers Sydney, Melbourne, and Auckland, and the OSM US New York-Chicago-Seattle civic amenities dataset counts the same trio inside exact city limits with a drinking-water-toilets-bench trio.
