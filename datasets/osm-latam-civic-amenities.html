OpenStreetMap Civic Amenities: São Paulo, Buenos Aires, Mexico City

385 nodes, 3 cities3 amenity types2026-09-10 countedfree CSV

One CSV, nine rows: São Paulo, Buenos Aires, and Mexico City × three civic amenity types. Each row is the count of OpenStreetMap nodes carrying that amenity tag inside the city's administrative boundary, queried from the Overpass API in one run, with the license line carried verbatim in a fourth column.

The amenity tags are amenity=drinking_water (fountains and public tap points), amenity=public_bookcase (street-side book-sharing shelves), and amenity=bicycle_repair_station (public stands with tools or an air pump). The query shape is area(id)->.a; nwr["amenity"="drinking_water"](area.a); out count;, one call per theme per city, area-filtered rather than bbox-filtered, so the numbers follow exact city limits.

Buenos Aires leads drinking water at 182 mapped nodes, followed by São Paulo at 105 and Mexico City at 61. The bookcase tag barely exists in Latin America so far: five nodes across all three cities, with Mexico City at zero (nobody has mapped one in the Distrito Federal boundary yet, not an absence of shelves). Bike repair stands concentrate in Mexico City, whose 23 stands are more than the other two cities combined.

Counts ran on 2026-09-10 against the Overpass API (kumi.systems mirror, keyless), so the numbers are a snapshot of a database that moves daily. Boundaries are Nominatim administrative relations (São Paulo R298285, Buenos Aires R3082668 for the Ciudad Autónoma, Mexico City R1376330), each passed to Overpass as its 3600000000-offset area ID.

For the same three amenity types elsewhere in the catalog, see the OSM US civic amenities dataset (New York, Los Angeles, Chicago, Austin, Seattle), the OSM Asia civic amenities dataset (Tokyo, Jakarta, Seoul), and the OSM EU civic amenities dataset (Berlin, Amsterdam, Paris, Madrid, Warsaw), and the OSM Africa civic amenities dataset (Lagos, Nairobi, Addis Ababa), the OSM Oceania civic amenities dataset (Sydney, Melbourne, Auckland, with a drinking-water-toilets-bench trio), and the OSM US civic amenities, New York-Chicago-Seattle dataset (exact city boundaries, drinking water, toilets, benches). For open housing data, the Eurostat house price index dataset covers EU-27 metro areas.
