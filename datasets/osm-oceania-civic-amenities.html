OpenStreetMap Civic Amenities: Sydney, Melbourne, Auckland

27,231 features, 3 cities3 amenity types2026-09-10 countedfree CSV

One CSV, nine rows: Sydney, Melbourne, and Auckland × three civic amenity types. Each row is the count of OpenStreetMap features carrying that amenity tag inside the city's boundary, queried from the Overpass API in one run, with the license line carried verbatim in a fourth column.

The amenity tags are amenity=drinking_water (fountains and public tap points), amenity=toilets (public and customer toilets, mapped as nodes and ways), and amenity=bench (park seats and bus-stop benches). This is the first region in the series to count toilets and benches; drinking water keeps the series' common theme so the numbers line up with the other six regions. The query shape is area(id)->.a; nwr["amenity"="toilets"](area.a); out count;, one call per theme per city, area-filtered rather than bbox-filtered, so the numbers follow exact city limits.

Sydney owns this comparison. 13,573 benches, 2,501 toilets, and 2,479 drinking-water points inside the metro boundary — the largest single-city count of any amenity in the whole series (the previous high was Nairobi's 2,174 water points). The toilet count is the useful one for trip planning: Sydney councils map operators, wheelchair access, and changing tables, and many nodes link back to the national toilet map (toiletmap.gov.au). Melbourne looks thin by comparison (355 drinking fountains, 142 toilets) because its boundary is the 37 km² City of Melbourne municipality, not greater Melbourne — its count includes heritage pieces like the Temperance Fountain. Auckland's 992 toilets and 4,916 benches sit between the two, under the unitary authority that spans the whole region.

Counts ran on 2026-09-10 against the Overpass API (maps.mail.ru mirror, keyless, base snapshot 2026-09-08T20:07:04Z), so the numbers are a snapshot of a database that moves daily. A parallel run on the overpass-api.de mirror matched all 9 counts exactly. Boundaries are Nominatim administrative relations, each passed to Overpass as its 3600000000-offset area ID: Sydney R5750005, City of Melbourne R2404870, and the Auckland unitary authority R2094141. A brief that circulated for this batch suggested Sydney R7003490; verification showed that relation is a suburb of Rzeszów, Poland, and it was discarded.

For civic amenity counts elsewhere in the catalog, see the OSM US civic amenities dataset (New York, Los Angeles, Chicago, Austin, Seattle), the OSM Asia civic amenities dataset (Tokyo, Jakarta, Seoul), the OSM EU civic amenities dataset (Berlin, Amsterdam, Paris, Madrid, Warsaw), the OSM Latin America civic amenities dataset (São Paulo, Buenos Aires, Mexico City), the OSM MENA civic amenities dataset (Istanbul, Cairo, Mumbai), and the OSM Africa civic amenities dataset (Lagos, Nairobi, Addis Ababa).
