OpenStreetMap Civic Amenities: New York, Chicago, Seattle

27,624 features, 3 cities3 amenity types2026-09-10 countedfree CSV

One CSV, nine rows: New York City, Chicago, and Seattle × three civic amenity types. Each row is the count of OpenStreetMap features carrying that amenity tag inside the city's exact boundary, queried from the Overpass API in one run, with the license line carried verbatim in a fourth column. An earlier US dataset on this site (five cities, bounding-box counts of fountains, bookcases, and repair stations) shares the three cities with this table; the themes and boundary method differ, and the series' recent regions use the same toilets-and-benches set as this one.

The amenity tags are amenity=drinking_water (fountains and tap points), amenity=toilets (public and customer facilities, mapped as nodes and building ways), and amenity=bench (park, transit, and street seats). The query shape is area(id)->.a; nwr["amenity"="bench"](area.a); out count;, one call per theme per city, so the numbers follow city limits and match the Sydney-Melbourne-Auckland table theme for theme.

New York's 18,991 mapped benches top the whole series by more than five thousand (Sydney's 13,573 held the record before), more than ten times Chicago's 1,905. NYC Parks maps drinking fountains and comfort stations systematically; the toilets sample carries operator=NYC Parks tags. Chicago's 252 fountains include sculptural pieces like the Vienna Drinking Fountain in Humboldt Park, and its toilet count is mostly Park District comfort stations mapped as buildings. Seattle's fountains are sparse at 193, but its 3,446 benches and 231 toilets carry the region's Parks and Recreation survey attributes, wheelchair flags included.

Counts ran on 2026-09-10 against the Overpass API (maps.mail.ru mirror, keyless, base snapshot 2026-09-08T21:00:20Z), so the numbers are a snapshot of a database that moves daily. A parallel run on the overpass-api.de mirror matched all 9 counts exactly. Boundaries are Nominatim administrative relations, each re-verified through OSM API tags and passed to Overpass as its 3600000000-offset area ID: New York City R175905 (City of New York, admin_level 5), Chicago R122604 (City of Chicago, admin_level 8), and Seattle R237385 (City of Seattle, admin_level 8). Draft R-ids that circulated for this batch (R2552842, R129221, R858925) failed tag verification, resolving to a Ukrainian village council, an Iowa town of 842 people, and an empty relation, and were discarded.

For civic amenity counts elsewhere in the catalog, see the OSM US civic amenities dataset (five cities, bounding-box counts), the OSM Oceania civic amenities dataset (Sydney, Melbourne, Auckland), the OSM Asia civic amenities dataset (Tokyo, Jakarta, Seoul), the OSM EU civic amenities dataset (Berlin, Amsterdam, Paris, Madrid, Warsaw), the OSM Latin America civic amenities dataset (São Paulo, Buenos Aires, Mexico City), the OSM MENA civic amenities dataset (Istanbul, Cairo, Mumbai), and the OSM Africa civic amenities dataset (Lagos, Nairobi, Addis Ababa).
