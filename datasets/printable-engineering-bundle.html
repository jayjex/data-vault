Printable Engineering Bundle

120 worksheet pages5 spreadsheet templatesA4 + US Letterone-time purchase

Two packs in one download: a full kindergarten worksheet book and a set of crypto bookkeeping spreadsheets. Print the worksheets for the kids, open the spreadsheets to log what the side hustle made. Both are build-it-once files, no subscriptions, no app.

Worksheets (120 pages, ages 4 to 6). Letter and number tracing, CVC word builds, sight word practice (Fry first 25, a public-domain list), counting, addition, 8 mazes, 8 dot-to-dots, patterns, and 12 coloring pages. Answer keys at the back, big print-friendly type, and one PDF per paper size so nothing gets scaled down. Print single-sided at 100% scale; black-and-white is fine.

Spreadsheets (5 templates, Google Sheets and Excel). Crypto portfolio tracker with allocation checks, airdrop and testnet tracker, DeFi yield log, bounty and gig income tracker with monthly SUMIF totals, and a trading journal with win rate and profit factor. Live formulas everywhere: add rows above the total row and the ranges stretch. Dropdowns for Status and Long/Short in the XLSX. Nothing pulls live prices; you type or paste them.

This page indexes the bundle; the files come from two packs that were already built, priced and QA'd, and no new files were produced for it. The free side of the shelf is the free printables index (223 pages), which links each funnel page that prints a free sample.
