World Bank Population, G20 Countries + EU27

1,320 rows20 entities66 yearsfree full data

Total population for the 19 national G20 members plus the European Union aggregate, from the World Bank's WDI indicator SP.POP.TOTL. One row per entity-year, 1960 through 2025, four columns: entity name, ISO3 code, year, and population. Every entity carries a complete 66-year run, so the file joins cleanly against any year-sliced series.

2025 ranking: India 1,463,865,525 leads China 1,406,585,000, and the crossover is visible in the data itself (India passed China in 2021). The EU27 aggregate (451,127,411) sits third and is marked EUU in the ISO3 column, so exclude it when summing national members; the 19 countries together count 4,744,611,663 people. Australia is the smallest member at 27,614,411.

Six of the twenty entities already peaked. Japan topped out in 2010 (128,070,000, now 123,366,734), Russia in 1992 (148,538,197, now 143,513,328), Italy in 2014, Korea in 2020, China in 2021 (1,412,360,000, now 1,406,585,000), Germany in 2024. On the other end, Saudi Arabia has multiplied 15.2x since 1960, the fastest growth in the set ahead of South Africa (3.9x) and Mexico (3.6x).

The source is one keyless call to the World Bank API v2, and the pack keeps the API's values verbatim: no rounding, no imputation, entity names as the Bank spells them ("Korea, Rep.", "Turkiye", "Russian Federation"). Years where the Bank reports no value are dropped, and there are none in this window. The full column dictionary and caveats are in schema.md.

Source: World Bank World Development Indicators via api.worldbank.org, indicator SP.POP.TOTL, retrieved 2026-09-10, WDI last updated 2026-07-13. License CC BY 4.0.
