HUD metro area list: every FY2026 metro FMR area

FY2026 edition, published 2026-09-10. Data source: HUD, public domain. Data Vault is not affiliated with HUD. This page documents the data; it is not housing or legal advice.

What counts as a HUD metro area

HUD publishes Fair Market Rents per FMR area, its own unit of geography, not per city or Census metro. The FY2026 ZIP-level file in the HUD FMR FY2026 pack carries 51,895 ZIP rows spread across 2,599 distinct area names. Counting the metro column: 649 metro areas hold 27,296 ZIP rows, and 1,950 nonmetro areas hold the other 24,599. A county rollup in the same pack splits 3,229 counties into 1,253 metro and 1,976 nonmetro.

The names are consistent once you see the pattern. Metro names end in HUD Metro FMR Area or MSA, nonmetro names in HUD Nonmetro FMR Area, and each name includes its state suffixes: "Abilene, TX MSA", "Washington-Arlington-Alexandria, DC-VA-MD HUD Metro FMR Area", "Yauco, PR HUD Metro FMR Area". The area_name column is the human-readable key; the hud_area_code column, METRO or NCNTY plus an area identifier, is the stable ID for joins. Why some metros end in MSA and others in HUD Metro FMR Area, with the 246/403 split and a worked San Diego example, is in HUD FMR by metro area.

HUD metro area questions

How many metro areas are in the HUD FMR data?
The FY2026 ZIP-level file uses 2,599 distinct HUD FMR area names: 649 metro areas holding 27,296 ZIP rows, plus 1,950 nonmetro areas covering the remaining 24,599 ZIPs. Every area name carries the suffix "HUD Metro FMR Area" or "MSA" in the metro case. A separate county rollup in the same pack covers 3,229 counties, 1,253 of them metro.
Which HUD metro areas cover the most ZIP codes?
Washington-Arlington-Alexandria, DC-VA-MD leads FY2026 with 488 ZIP rows, followed by Los Angeles-Long Beach-Glendale, CA (474) and Philadelphia-Camden-Wilmington, PA-NJ-DE-MD (473). New York, NY covers 410 ZIPs and Pittsburgh, PA covers 393. All ten largest metros are in the table on this page with their 2BR medians and per-ZIP ranges.
Why do HUD metro area names differ from Census metro names?
HUD defines its own FMR areas rather than adopting Census or OMB boundaries, so the same city can carry a different name and footprint. In the FY2026 file, New York is "New York, NY HUD Metro FMR Area" while the Census equivalent is the New York-Newark-Jersey City MSA covering parts of NY and NJ. Use the area_name column as the lookup key and the hud_area_code column (METRO or NCNTY plus an area identifier) as the stable ID when joining across tables.
