Istanbul drinking water fountains, Cairo and Mumbai public amenities: counted from open data

Planning an Istanbul drinking water fountains map, hunting Cairo public amenities open data, or plotting Mumbai water fountain locations all hit the same wall: no city publishes a plain count of what exists. OpenStreetMap does carry the tags point by point (amenity=drinking_water, amenity=public_bookcase, amenity=bicycle_repair_station), but reading a tile map gives you a picture, not a number. This guide counts those three tags inside the exact administrative boundaries of Istanbul, Cairo, and Mumbai and ships the result as free CSV: 359 features across the three cities. The dataset page has the per-row preview and sample files; the worldwide 16-city table puts these counts next to New York, Tokyo, Madrid, and eleven more cities.

Three cities, three tags, 359 features

Every number is the count of OSM features carrying that tag inside the city boundary on the snapshot date, nodes plus ways. Istanbul covers the full province, Mumbai its two districts, Cairo the governorate:

Istanbul: 194 fountains, most of them Ottoman çeşme

Anyone building an Istanbul drinking water fountains map works with a dataset dominated by history rather than street furniture: the drinking_water tag in this province attaches overwhelmingly to Ottoman-era public fountains (çeşme), like the Siyavuşpaşa Çeşmesi (node 1026153699) and the Hatice Turhan Valide Sultan Çeşmesi (node 5221026261) in the old city. One caution before you zoom out: these counts cover the whole İstanbul province (relation R223474), which stretches past the built-up city into rural Marmara and Black Sea districts, and the historic-fountain mapping enthusiasm inflates the total relative to a strictly urban map. A fountain-hunting app for tourists should filter by coordinates first. The single public bookcase (a way in Kadıköy) and 16 bike repair stations, including Yusuf Bisiklet in Kadıköy and Bike City near the old peninsula, round out the 211 features.

Cairo: 78 fountains and the honest zeros

Cairo public amenities open data starts with what the OSM database actually holds: 78 drinking_water features inside the Cairo Governorate boundary (relation R5466227) and zeros for public bookcases and bike repair stations. The zeros are real query results, not missing data. Nobody has mapped a single bookcase or repair stand inside the governorate yet, so a Cairo layer for either amenity starts empty, and the fix is fieldwork, not code. The fountains that do exist skew toward named commercial water points rather than street fountains, like the drinking water company node at 30.0536, 31.3339 downtown. For a count this thin, the boundary matters: governorate limits exclude Giza and the rest of Greater Cairo, so a metro-wide map would need those areas queried separately.

Mumbai: 67 fountain locations and a 27-point cluster

Mumbai water fountain locations are counted across both city districts, Mumbai Suburban (R7964375) and Mumbai City (R7964376), because the city-level relation has no Overpass area. The 67 drinking_water features include mapped public pyau (water fountains), such as the Durgadevi Saraf Pyau (node 12293013067) and Ghanshyamdas Saraf Pyau (node 12298863511), with one dense W1-W27 cluster of 27 stations sitting around a single building at 19.133, 72.915, worth deduplicating before plotting a walking route. A bounding-box cross-check counts 71 fountains over the same ground; the four-fountain gap is the harbor edge clipping differently, and the area-based 67 is the number this file uses. Three bicycle repair stations (Sagar Cycle among them) complete the 70.

Method and snapshot date

All counts ran through the Overpass API (kumi.systems mirror, keyless) with out count queries, one per city per tag, inside Nominatim-verified administrative areas. The mirror's base timestamp is 2026-07-28, so the numbers are a snapshot of a database that changes daily. Nodes and ways count; relations are ignored (none carry these tags in the three areas). Full boundary notes, example nodes with coordinates, and the Mumbai bbox cross-check live in the schema.md next to the CSV.
