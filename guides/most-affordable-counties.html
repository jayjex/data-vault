Most affordable counties to rent in, by HUD FMR

FY2026 county edition, published 2026-09-10. Data source: HUD, public domain. Data Vault is not affiliated with HUD. This page documents the data; it is not housing or financial advice.

What affordable means here

Every number on this page comes from HUD's FY2026 county Fair Market Rent table: 3,229 counties and county equivalents, each with median and max rents for studios through 4-bedroom units. FMR is HUD's estimate of the 40th percentile gross rent, shelter rent plus utilities, for standard-quality units in a local market. A county with a low FMR has cheap rents by that measure. It says nothing about local incomes, which is where the rent-to-income check comes in.

The national county median for a 2BR is $1,018. At the top, Santa Cruz County, CA asks $4,214. At the bottom, a cluster of Puerto Rico municipios sits at $475. The ranking below works through that spread from the floor up.

The typical county charges 1.95 times as much for a 4BR as for a studio. Individual counties run from 1.52 to 2.43 on that ratio, and the spread matters more the bigger the household, which is the subject of a section below.

The cheapest counties are all Puerto Rico

Sorted by 2BR median, the bottom of the file is a wall of $475: Arroyo, Coamo, Culebra, Guayama, Guánica, Jayuya, Maricao, Patillas, Salinas, Santa Isabel and Vieques all tie there. The next tiers are $486 and $493, then $504 and $510, so the island's floor is not one lucky row but a band of municipios within about 7 percent of each other. For a studio, the floor drops to $407 in Cabo Rojo, Lajas and Sabana Grande.

HUD measures local rents, and Puerto Rico's rental market prices far below the mainland, so low FMRs track the market rather than flag a data error. But cheap rent is cheap only relative to what you earn, and HUD's rent table carries no income data. Pair the county file with household income before calling any county affordable for you.

Cheapest on the mainland: 19 Alabama counties at $776

Drop Puerto Rico and the floor lands on Alabama. Nineteen counties tie at a 2BR median of $776, and 18 of them are nonmetro. The next values up are $778, $783 and $791, and the 25 cheapest mainland counties are all Alabama before any other state appears.

For voucher households, that $776 2BR FMR is the base for the payment standard, the 90 to 110 percent band HUD agencies set around it: roughly $698 to $853 in a county like this. The band math and the move-in rules are in the payment standard guide.

Geography explains most of the gap between the halves. Metro counties carry a 2BR median of $1,276 against $973 in nonmetro counties, and 2,429 of the 3,229 counties price below that metro median. The metro-side ranking, where the expensive half lives, is in FMR by metro: the priciest and cheapest metros.

Cheap 2BR, but check the 4BR before you move the family

The 4BR-to-studio ratio averages 1.95x across the file. Among the 100 cheapest counties by 2BR, the median ratio is 1.86x, and among the 100 most expensive it is 1.89x, so cheap markets are, if anything, slightly flatter. Two outliers show the range. Calhoun County, FL is the flattest in the file at 1.52x, with a studio at $848 and a 4BR at $1,288. Linn County, IA is the steepest at 2.43x: a 2BR median of $738 reads as cheap, yet the 4BR median of $1,797 is more than double the studio price of $738.

The practical version: screen on the bedroom size you actually need, not the 2BR column alone, when the household is larger than two.

Run the screen yourself

The full county table is a free CSV, 3,229 rows, no paywall, since the source is US government public domain data. Load it in a spreadsheet, sort the fmr_2br_median column, and the tables above reproduce in minutes. The HUD FMR by county guide walks the columns and shows an MCP query for a single county if you would rather filter than download, and the 30 percent rule calculator tests any of these rents against an income.

The same public-data method, applied to a different question, drives the Tokyo, Jakarta and Seoul civic amenities dataset: count what a place actually has, from OpenStreetMap, and compare cities on the counts instead of the marketing. Cheap counties and amenity-dense cities are opposite ends of one tradeoff, and having both datasets in one catalog makes the tradeoff checkable.

Affordable counties questions

What is the cheapest county to rent in per HUD data?
Eleven Puerto Rico municipios tie at the bottom of the FY2026 county file with a 2BR FMR of 475 dollars: Arroyo, Coamo, Culebra, Guayama, Guánica, Jayuya, Maricao, Patillas, Salinas, Santa Isabel and Vieques. On the mainland, 19 Alabama counties tie at 776 dollars for a 2BR, all but one of them nonmetro.
How many counties have a 2BR FMR under 900 dollars?
483 of the 3,229 counties in the FY2026 county file sit at or under 900 dollars for a 2BR, about 15 percent. 1,515 counties sit at or under 1,000 dollars, which is just below the national county median of 1,018.
Why does the bedroom ladder matter when you compare counties?
The typical county charges 1.95 times as much for a 4BR as for a studio, but the county-level ratio runs from 1.52 to 2.43. Linn County, Iowa shows why the range matters: its 2BR median of 738 dollars looks cheap, yet its 4BR median of 1,797 dollars is the steepest climb in the file at 2.43 times its studio price. A cheap 2BR market can still be relatively pricey for a family-sized unit.
