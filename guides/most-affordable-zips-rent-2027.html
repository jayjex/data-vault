The most affordable US ZIP codes for renters in 2027, by HUD fair market rent

FY2027 edition, published 2026-09-10. Data source: HUD FY2027 Fair Market Rent release, effective October 1, 2026. License: CC0 1.0 (US government data, public domain). Data Vault is not affiliated with HUD. This page documents the data; it is not housing or legal advice.

The short answer

The cheapest 2-bedroom rent HUD projects for FY2027 is 890 dollars a month, and it is not a single ZIP code: 133 distinct ZIP codes share the floor, spread across 46 HUD areas in South Carolina and Louisiana. By ZIP number, the lowest is 29001 in Clarendon County, SC, which heads the table below. The list comes from the FY2027 ZIP-level fair market rent dataset, all 51,871 rows recomputed from the raw file.

This is the mirror of the most-expensive-metros ranking, where Santa Cruz-Watsonville tops the list at a 4,250-dollar 2BR median. The same file bottoms out 5.9 times lower at the 890-dollar floor. One caveat before the table: Puerto Rico is excluded from every ranking on this page. Its 216 rows price from 520 to 1,060 dollars for a 2BR, so left in, one territory would sweep the entire bottom of a US list. The methodology section has the numbers.

Methodology: the Puerto Rico exclusion

The FY2027 file holds 51,871 rows, 216 of them Puerto Rico. Puerto Rico's 2BR rows run from 520 dollars (ZIP 00669, Aguadilla) to 1,060, with a 610-dollar median, so on an unfiltered ranking every one of the top slots would be Puerto Rico. That is a true fact about the file and a useless answer to "where can I rent cheap in the US," because the territory prices its own market under different conditions. Every table on this page therefore ranks the 50 states and DC only: 51,655 rows over 38,432 distinct ZIP codes. The Puerto Rico rows stay in the downloadable CSV untouched, so the all-rows floor of 520 still belongs to Aguadilla, PR, the number the dataset page reports.

Two more conventions worth knowing before recomputing. First, 10,093 ZIPs carry more than one row because HUD splits some ZIPs between metro and nonmetro areas, so a per-ZIP ranking needs a dedup; the query below keeps each ZIP's cheapest row and breaks ties on area name. Second, all figures on this page are FMRs, HUD's rent estimates for the coming fiscal year effective October 1, 2026, not observed asking rents, and they are set at the 40th percentile of local rents, so they describe what a modest rental costs, not the market floor in cash terms.

Recompute the ranking yourself

Load the ZIP-level CSV into SQLite and the whole bottom-12 falls out of one query. The dedup window function keeps the cheapest row per ZIP, the CAST keeps the ZIP order numeric, and the WHERE clause applies the Puerto Rico exclusion:

-- SQLite: the 12 cheapest ZIP codes by 2BR FMR, Puerto Rico excluded
SELECT zip, area_name, state, fmr_0br, fmr_1br, fmr_2br, fmr_3br, fmr_4br
FROM (
  SELECT *, row_number() OVER (
    PARTITION BY zip
    ORDER BY fmr_2br, area_name
  ) AS rn
  FROM zips
  WHERE state <> 'PR'
)
WHERE rn = 1
ORDER BY fmr_2br, CAST(zip AS INTEGER)
LIMIT 12;
-- 12 rows, every fmr_2br = 890: 29001, 29038, 29041, 29042, 29046,
-- 29048, 29080, 29081, 29115, 29117, 29133, 29148, all South Carolina

Run it against the table built by the SQL guide's import block and the output matches the first table row for row, including the all-South-Carolina tail. Drop the LIMIT or re-order on any bedroom column to extend the table; sort without the state filter and Puerto Rico's 520-dollar rows take over the top of the list, which is exactly why the exclusion is stated up front.

Affordable ZIP codes 2027: questions

What is the cheapest ZIP code for rent in the US in 2027?
ZIP 29001 in Clarendon County, SC carries the lowest 2BR fair market rent of any mainland ZIP in the FY2027 file at 890 dollars, and it shares that floor with 132 other ZIP codes, 133 in all across 46 HUD areas in South Carolina and Louisiana. The floor moved up from 780 dollars in FY2026, spread over 28 Alabama county areas from Talladega to Choctaw.
Which state has the cheapest fair market rent in 2027?
Arkansas and Mississippi tie for the cheapest state-level 2BR median at 960 dollars, followed by Oklahoma at 970 and Kentucky and West Virginia at 980. California sits at the other end at 2,600, 2.7 times the Arkansas median. The cheapest full metro area in the file is Gadsden, AL at a 920-dollar 2BR median across its 17 ZIP rows.
Why does this ranking exclude Puerto Rico?
Puerto Rico runs its own housing market at a different price level: its 216 rows in the FY2027 file carry 2BR rents from 520 to 1,060 dollars with a 610-dollar median, so including it would fill the whole bottom of a US ranking with one territory. The ranking covers the 50 states and DC, and the Puerto Rico rows stay in the downloadable file for anyone who wants them.
