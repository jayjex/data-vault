Most expensive US metro areas for rent in 2027, by HUD fair market rent

FY2027 edition, published 2026-09-10. Data source: HUD FY2027 Fair Market Rent release, effective October 1, 2026. US government data, public domain. Data Vault is not affiliated with HUD. This page documents the data; it is not housing or legal advice.

The short answer

Santa Cruz-Watsonville, CA is the most expensive US metro area for rent in FY2027, with a 2BR fair market rent median of 4,250 dollars, ahead of San Francisco at 3,590 and New York at 3,295. The HUD FMR metro 2027 dataset ranks all 649 metro FMR areas by 2BR price and ships the top 50 as a free CSV. Ten of the twelve priciest metros are in California; New York and Boston are the only entries from anywhere else.

Price and ZIP coverage are two different rankings, and they crown different leaders. The same pack ranks the 50 metro areas with the most ZIP codes, and Washington DC tops that table with 488 ZIPs at a 2,400-dollar 2BR median, 35th on price. Only 12 areas appear in both top-50s.

Santa Cruz-Watsonville leads at every bedroom size: its studio median of 3,230 beats New York's 2,865, and its 4BR median of 5,740 beats San Francisco's 5,280. The #1 spot held from the FY2026 edition of this ranking, but the gap moved. Santa Cruz's 2BR median slipped from 4,350 to 4,250, while San Francisco rose from 3,480 to 3,590 and New York from 3,175 to 3,295. Boston, San Diego-Chula Vista-Carlsbad and Santa Rosa-Petaluma tie at 2,820 for the last three slots in the table, with Lowell, MA and Oakland-Fremont one dollar behind at 2,810 and Jersey City at 2,790.

Washington DC: big coverage, mid-table price

Washington-Arlington-Alexandria spans 488 ZIP rows in the FY2027 file, more than any other metro, and its 2BR median of 2,400 dollars ranks 35th on price. A Santa Cruz studio (3,230) outprices a DC 3-bedroom (3,060). Los Angeles-Long Beach-Glendale runs 474 ZIPs at 2,960 (9th on price) and Philadelphia-Camden-Wilmington 473 at 1,900. Together the 50 coverage metros carry 10,143 of the 27,288 metro-assigned ZIPs in the FY2027 file, yet only 12 of them also make the price top 50. The cheapest 2BR in the coverage table, Tulsa at 1,150, prices at 3.7 times less than Santa Cruz's 4,250.

The tail of the price list shows the same spread from the other end. California places 16 metros in the top 50, Massachusetts 8 and New Jersey 7, and Nashua, NH closes the list at 2,160. Recompute the ranking yourself

The metro dataset page ships most-expensive-2br.csv with all 50 rows pre-sorted, and the FY2027 guide reads the ZIP-level file these medians roll up from. Four lines of Python re-derive the headline from the price table:

import csv

rows = sorted(csv.DictReader(open("most-expensive-2br.csv")),
              key=lambda r: -int(r["fmr_2br_median"]))
print(rows[0]["metro_name"], rows[0]["fmr_2br_median"])
# Santa Cruz-Watsonville, CA MSA 4250

Swap the sort key for any bedroom column to re-rank. Every metro row in the two tables reproduces the same way, and the median-of-ZIPs convention is documented in the pack's schema.

Most expensive metros 2027: questions

Which US metro area has the highest rent in 2027?
Santa Cruz-Watsonville, CA tops the FY2027 ranking with a 2BR fair market rent median of 4,250 dollars across its 23 ZIP rows, and its studio median of 3,230 and 4BR median of 5,740 also lead every metro. San Francisco follows at 3,590, New York at 3,295, San Jose-Sunnyvale-Santa Clara at 3,270 and Napa at 3,200.
Does Washington DC have the highest fair market rent?
No. Washington-Arlington-Alexandria covers 488 ZIP rows, the most of any metro, but its 2BR median of 2,400 dollars ranks 35th on price. Ranking by ZIP coverage and ranking by price produce different leaders: Santa Cruz-Watsonville carries 23 ZIPs at 4,250 dollars, and only 12 areas appear in both of the pack's top-50 tables.
How many metro areas does HUD set fair market rents for?
The FY2027 file covers 649 metro FMR areas. The metro pack ships the 50 priciest by 2BR median, from Santa Cruz-Watsonville at 4,250 dollars down to Nashua, NH at 2,160, plus the 50 largest by ZIP count. California places 16 metros in the price top 50, Massachusetts 8 and New Jersey 7.
