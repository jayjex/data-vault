NFL point spread history: 27 seasons of closing lines, counted

Counted from the NFL Games & Betting Lines 1999-2026 pack, file games.csv, 7,548 rows · the spread_line column explained in the spread history CSV guide · raw file sources in the NFL data APIs comparison. Nothing on this page is betting advice.

What was counted, and how

The games.csv file holds 7,548 NFL games from the 1999 season through the 2026 schedule. The spread_line column, the closing point spread, exists on 7,388 of them: every played game plus 112 scheduled 2026 games that already carry early numbers. This page counts historical patterns on the 7,276 games that have both a final score and a line, and it treats the sign convention the file uses: a positive spread_line means the home team is favored by that many points, negative means the visitor is favored, zero is a pick'em.

Every number below was computed by running one pass over those 7,276 rows and the 7,388 rows with a line. No season-summaries aggregates were copied except where stated, and where they were, the row count checks out.

Average NFL spread by era

Take the absolute value of every closing line and average it by five-year buckets. Lines have drifted down, from a 5.3-5.5 point plateau through the 2010s to under 5 in the current era:

Per season, the widest book was 2009 at 6.54 points and the tightest full season was 2016 at 4.63. The median line across all 7,388 rows is 4.0, so half the games in NFL history since 1999 have closed at a field goal or less. The 2024-2026 bucket includes 112 scheduled 2026 games carrying early numbers rather than closed lines, which pulls its average down a little; on played 2024-2025 games alone it sits near 5.1.

How often the home team is favored

Home teams were favored in 4,731 of 7,276 played games, 65.0%. Visitors were favored in 2,514 (34.6%) and 31 games closed on the pick'em, spread_line exactly 0. Counting the 112 scheduled 2026 rows with early lines moves the home-favorite share to 65.1% (4,806 of 7,388), which tells you the ratio holds in the current schedule too.

The lines themselves are lopsided in the same direction. When the home team is favored, the average line is 5.82 points; when the visitor is favored, the average is 4.47. Bookmakers also charge extra for the jersey: the average signed spread_line across all rows is +2.24 for the home side, which matches the 2.39-point scoring gap the home field advantage guide measures directly from the scores.

Spread accuracy: how often the favorite covers

A favorite covers when it wins by more than the line. Running that over all 7,276 rows:

OutcomeGamesShare
Favorite covered3,46448.9% of decided games
Underdog covered3,61751.1% of decided games
Push (margin exactly equals the line)1952.7% of all games

Over the 7,081 games decided against the number, favorites went 3,464-3,617, a 48.9% cover rate. One counting choice is worth stating: on the 31 pick'ems there is no favorite by the spread, so the favorite side was read off the moneyline for those rows; score-only tie-breaking instead gives favorites 3,473 covers, 49.0%. Either way the point spread lands within half a point of a coin flip, which is what a closing line is built to do, and the 195 pushes reconcile exactly with the shipped season-summaries.csv.

The season table in the spread history CSV guide breaks this per season from the shipped file. Extremes there: favorites covered 58.8% in 2005, their best run in the file, and 43.6% in 2006, their worst, a full swing of 15 points in one offseason with no structural change in the data.

NFL point spread history questions

What is the biggest NFL point spread in history?

Counted from the games CSV, the biggest closing spread since 1999 is 27 points: Denver favored at home over Jacksonville on October 13, 2013. Denver won 35-19 and failed to cover. Next come New England -24 over Philadelphia (November 25, 2007, won by 3, no cover) and two 22-point lines, New England -22 over Miami (December 23, 2007) and Dallas -22 over Miami (September 22, 2019). Only 9 of 7,276 played games ever carried a closing line of 20 points or more, and the favorites went 3-6 against the number on them.

What percentage of NFL favorites cover the spread?

Across the 7,276 played games from 1999 through 2025 that carry a closing line, favorites covered 3,464 times and failed 3,617, with 195 pushes. That is a 48.9% cover rate over the 7,081 decided games, counted from the rows with the favorite side read off spread_line and, on the 31 pick'ems, off the moneyline. Counting pick'ems by score instead gives favorites 3,473 covers, 49.0%. The 2.7% push rate matches the shipped season-summaries.csv, and underdogs took the cover count in more seasons than favorites.

How often is the home team the favorite in the NFL?

In 65.0% of played games since 1999: 4,731 of the 7,276 games with a closing line have a positive spread_line, which by the file's sign convention means the home team is favored. Road teams were favored in 2,514 (34.6%) and 31 games (0.4%) closed as pick'ems. When the home team is favored its average line is 5.82 points; when the visitor is favored, the average is 4.47 points. That asymmetry is the market pricing home field, which the home field advantage guide counts at about 2.4 points per game.
