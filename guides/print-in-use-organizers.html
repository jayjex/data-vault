3D printed desk organizers and printable tools: 10 parametric models

Published 2026-09-10. All models on the MakerWorld @jayjey profile, license CC BY 4.0. Page by Data Vault.

Ten parametric 3D printing models, generated as code and uploaded to our MakerWorld profile. The set covers desk organizers and printable tools: an adhesive cable clip set, a two-piece phone stand, a wall-mount bit holder, two rulers, a bag clip, a hex key gauge, and a few household helpers. One model is live on MakerWorld and nine sat in MakerWorld's review queue when this page was published, so their model pages will appear as review clears. Everything here prints flat on the plate with no supports, and every generator ships with the model files so you can rebuild any part at your own dimensions.

One thing to know before you download: these are renders and mesh-checked geometry, not test prints. Each mesh passed watertight and manifold checks before upload, but no filament has run through them yet. Print at your own settings, and the model descriptions on MakerWorld say the same thing.

What parametric means here

Each model ships with its generator script in the files. The cable clip generator takes cable diameter, wall thickness, clearance, and wall height. The seed ruler takes length, both notch pitches, and notch depth. The hex gauge takes the size list and clearance. Change the parameters, rerun the script, and you get a rebuilt STL and 3MF sized for your cables, drawer, or seed packets. Every mesh that came off these generators was checked watertight and manifold before upload. The parametric tools guide lists each model's rebuildable parameters side by side.

Flat prints, no supports

Every part in the set sits flat on the print plate. Walls are vertical, cutouts open upward or straight through, and nothing overhangs, so there is no support material and no cleanup pass. The cable clip plate weighs about 2.5 g in PLA; the hex key gauge comes to 17.4 cm³ solid. Wall widths target a standard 0.4 mm nozzle: the clips run 2.4 mm walls, which is three perimeters.
