Every printables page, one line each

The printables shelf: eight 3D print STL spec sheets plus the printable-templates funnel pages, one line per page. Every spec number is measured from the mesh with trimesh, and none of the models has been test-printed yet.

3D print STL spec sheets

  • Cable Clip Set STL: 4 Screw-Down Sizes for 4 to 9 mm Cables Four screw-down clips on one plate for 4, 5.5, 7 and 9 mm cables, 67.5 × 11 × 13.3 mm laid out, 3.8 cm³ of PLA, about a 25 minute print with no supports.
  • Phone Stand STL: Fold-Flat with a Print-in-Place Pin Hinge Prints in one pass with a 4 mm print-in-place pin hinge, folds to 91.5 × 72 × 16.9 mm for travel, 55.6 cm³ of PLA, about a 2.3 hour print.
  • Ruler Stencil STL: 15 cm Ruler with Circle and Slot Cutouts Millimeter and centimeter ticks on the bottom edge, inch ticks to 6 in on top, a 40 × 6 mm slot and two circle cutouts for stenciling; 160 × 32 × 3.5 mm, 15.4 cm³, about a 0.8 hour print, no supports.
  • Opisometer STL: Screw-Type Map Distance Tool, 3 Printed Parts A 16 mm wheel rolls a map route and screws itself down a 2.5 mm pitch thread, so wheel turns × 20.1 = route length; three parts, no fasteners, 3.3 cm³ of PLA, about half an hour of printing.
  • Insect Pinning Block STL: 24/16/8 mm Stages, Pin Guide Holes One piece at 72 × 46 × 24 mm with stage tops at 24, 16 and 8 mm, a Ø1.5 mm guide hole per stage for size 0-5 pins and three spare-pin pockets; prints flat, 52.5 cm³, about 2.2 hours.
  • Insect Pinning Block STL: 3 Step Heights, Parametric The compact sibling: three 22 × 20 mm towers on a 6 mm base, step tops at 30, 22 and 14 mm, a Ø0.65 mm guide hole per step through to the bench for #3 pins; prints flat, 32.3 cm³, under an hour.
  • Hotend Nozzle Dock STL: 8 Bays for MK8 and V6, No Supports Two rows of four tapered bays on a 77 × 39 × 24 mm slab, front row MK8 and back row V6, grip necks cut to the thread crest plus 0.4 mm; blind holes print flat with no supports, 64.1 cm³, about 2.7 hours.
  • Parametric Planisphere STL: Star Wheel for 45°N, 3 Parts A star wheel with 416 naked-eye star holes sized by magnitude on a barbed snap-pin pivot, a true-horizon window for 45°N and a snap-on hour ring; three parts, no fasteners, 13.7 cm³, about an hour.

Printable templates

  • Free Printables vs Paid Packs: When Free Is Enough and When a Bundle Wins 223 free sheets cover one-off jobs like a bill tracker or a rainy-day maze, bundles cover volume like 120 kindergarten worksheets with answer keys.
